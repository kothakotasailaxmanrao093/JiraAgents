# Live test plan — 21 test cases

Project: **BGV** (Praman) on jiraagentdemo.atlassian.net
Versions under test: router **0.1.24**, Work Breakdown **4.3.37**, Requirement Review **2.2.18**

---

## Before you start

1. **Aetherion → Agents → Agent List** shows the three versions above. Do not test until it does.
2. Every ticket you create is **Work type: Story** (never Epic or Sub-task).
3. Post comments in the normal **Add a comment** box unless a test says **Reply**.
4. Wait the time given, then refresh. Builds of big requirements take 3–4 minutes.
5. Epics are hidden in the list view — open them by key (`/browse/BGV-…`) to check or delete.

## Checks on every reply

| # | Check |
|---|---|
| A | The reply is **under your comment, in its thread** |
| B | **One** reply per comment |
| C | Starts `AetherionAgent · <headline>`, ends `— AetherionAgent · automated reply` |
| D | "Handled by" shows **Work Breakdown** / **Requirement Review**, never an internal name |
| E | No run id, no `Status :` / `Generated by :` lines |
| F | Email: **clarification, duplicates, failed** only — never on creation, review, help or invalid |

## Two quality numbers (record for every build and review)

- **Avoidable** — Work Breakdown's fault: a fact you wrote is missing from the tickets, a question about something you already said, an untestable criterion, "As a system".
- **Over-reach** — Review's fault: a finding about something your text never raised (API, database, SMTP, queues…), or asking for something the ticket already has.

## 16. A detailed requirement — every fact carried

**Ticket:** new Story — Summary `Monthly client invoicing`
**Description:**
```
Goal: bill each client every month for the background checks we completed for them.

Who uses it:
- Billing officer: reviews and sends invoices
- Client billing contact: receives and pays invoices
- Account manager: follows up unpaid invoices

Invoice lines:
- Every background check marked Completed adds one line to that client's invoice for the month it was completed.
- Each line shows: candidate name, check type (Employment, Education, Address, Criminal), completion date and price.
- Prices come from the client's contract price list. If a check type has no price in the contract, the line is marked "Price missing" and the invoice cannot be sent until a billing officer adds the price.

Monthly run:
- On the 1st of every month at 06:00 IST, the system creates one invoice per client for the previous month.
- A client with no completed checks in that month gets no invoice.
- Each invoice gets a unique number in the format INV-YYYYMM-<client code>, e.g. INV-202609-ACME.
- If the monthly run is started again for the same month, invoices that already exist are not created twice.

Sending:
- The invoice is a PDF attached to an email sent to every billing contact on the client's record.
- Email subject: "Invoice <invoice number> for <month year>". Email body: client name, invoice number, total amount, due date and a link to the client portal.
- Due date is 30 days after the invoice date.
- If a client has no billing contact email on record, the invoice is not sent; it appears in the billing officer's "Not sent" list with the reason "No billing contact".
- If an email bounces, the invoice is marked "Delivery failed" and appears in the same "Not sent" list. The billing officer can resend it after fixing the address.
- A billing officer can resend any invoice manually; each resend is recorded with date, time and who resent it.

Client portal:
- Client users can see all invoices from the last 24 months, newest first, and download each one as a PDF.
- Each invoice shows its status: Unpaid, Paid or Overdue.

Unpaid invoices:
- An invoice not paid by its due date becomes Overdue.
- 30 days after the due date, if still unpaid, one reminder email goes to the billing contacts and the client's account manager gets a task "Follow up overdue invoice <invoice number>".
- Only one reminder is sent per invoice.
- When a billing officer marks an invoice Paid, no further reminders are sent.

Out of scope:
- Online card payment
- Currencies other than INR
- Credit notes and refunds
```
**Comment:** `@Aetherion build`  **Wait:** 3–4 min

**Pass — open the Stories and check each fact is inside:**

| Fact | Where |
|---|---|
| "Price missing" blocks sending | invoice-lines Story |
| 06:00 IST · no invoice without checks · INV-YYYYMM · **never created twice** | monthly-run Story |
| **every** billing contact · subject/body · **due 30 days after the invoice date** · "Not sent" · "Delivery failed" · **resend recorded with date, time and who** | sending Story |
| 24 months newest first · Unpaid / Paid / Overdue | portal Story |
| reminder 30 days after due · **only one reminder** · account manager task · Paid stops reminders | overdue Story |
| **Nothing built** for card payment, other currencies, credit notes | (Epic's Out-of-scope field only) |

Also: "Details I could not determine" says **Nothing was missing** (or genuine gaps only); **no** "Please check: Line … does not carry it".
