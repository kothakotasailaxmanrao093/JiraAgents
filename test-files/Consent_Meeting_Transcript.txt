Meeting: Consent lifecycle — decisions
Date: 24 September 2026
Attendees: Priya (Product), Arjun (Verification Ops), Meera (Engineering)

Priya: Candidates keep asking how to take consent back. Let's decide it today.
Arjun: They can withdraw, but only before any check has started. Once a check is running, the policy says no.
Meera: Where do they do it?
Priya: In the candidate portal. A "Withdraw consent" button on their profile.
Arjun: When someone withdraws, the recruiter must be told within 1 hour.
Meera: Every consent event — given, renewed, withdrawn, expired — gets logged with date, time and IP address.
Priya: Agreed. Paper consent forms are out of scope for this release.
Arjun: One thing we did not settle: whether the client should also be notified when a candidate withdraws.
Priya: Leave that open for now.
